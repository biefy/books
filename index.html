Hands-on, experiment-driven books on Linux kernel internals.

Linux Network Subsystem in 30 Days

The kernel network stack from sk_buff to MPTCP. RX/TX paths, netfilter, conntrack, traffic control, kTLS, the modern features. Each day points at specific kernel files and functions.

30 days · 51 diagrams · ~20k words · verified against kernel 7.1

Practical eBPF in 30 Days

Modern eBPF on Linux 7.1. libbpf + CO-RE workflow, the verifier, tracing with fentry/fexit, networking BPF, kfuncs, struct_ops, and sched_ext. No bcc, no legacy.

30 days · 140 diagrams · ~130k words · capstone project on day 30
Read order: the network book first, then eBPF. The BPF book assumes you know the kernel network stack — that's what BPF programs hook into.
